What it does

Stays on the laptop
Recordings save to the laptop's own drive. SpareEye uploads nothing and works offline.
Clips on motion
Each motion event saves a 15-second clip that includes the 3 seconds before it, plus a photo. Clips older than 30 days are deleted automatically, or you can keep everything.
Telegram alerts
Optional. SpareEye sends a photo through your own bot, and this is the only feature that needs internet.
Any camera
Use the built-in webcam, a USB webcam, or an RTSP or HTTP network camera. Night hours get their own motion sensitivity.

How it works

  1. Install SpareEye and choose a camera.
  2. Connect your own Telegram bot, if you want alerts.
  3. Press Start watching. It starts at login and lives in the tray.

Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes, only Telegram alerts need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No, and Telegram uses a bot that you create.

Which computers are supported?

Windows 10 and 11, Debian, Ubuntu and other Linux, but not macOS yet.

Does it cost anything?

No, it is free and open source under the MIT licence.
