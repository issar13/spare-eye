Connect a Local Camera

            

                

                    
Plugged into USB Port

                    
Plug any standard USB webcam into your laptop. SpareEye finds driverless UVC cameras through the system's own camera drivers, so there is nothing else to install.

                    
Built-in webcamsLogitech seriesGeneric USB UVCHDMI-to-USB capture

                    
Only cameras that actually send a picture are listed. A webcam that is switched off, or already in use by another app, will not appear.

Local Home Wi-Fi / RTSP

                    
Connect any IP camera on your home Wi-Fi using its local stream address. Traffic stays on your network.

                    

                        
                        

                            
                            
                        

                        

                            
                            
                        

                    

                    

                    

                        Find the camera's device address (ONVIF search)
                        
The camera must have ONVIF enabled and be on the same local network. Search finds the device address, not the stream address — the stream address is in your camera's settings page or manual.
