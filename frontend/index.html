When your computer starts

                


                
Kaa Rada cannot see anything while it is closed. These two are separate
                    on purpose: one opens the app, the other turns the camera on.


                

                    

                    

                    
When your computer opens Kaa Rada for you, the window stays tucked
                        away in the taskbar. Open it whenever you want to look.
